**Project Eschaton** is an advanced 3D simulation engine that models the recursive collapse and recovery of a society under the pressure of biological threats. By integrating a **Kinematics Physics Engine** with a **Macro-Economic Suite**, it visualizes the delicate balance between public health, economic supply chains, and scientific research.

## 🛠 Features

### ☣️ Multi-Strain Viral Logic
* **Strain A & B:** Competing pathogens with different infection radii and mortality rates.
* **Waning Immunity:** Recovered agents slowly lose immunity over time, leading to natural, cyclical infection waves.
* **Bio-Hazard Zones:** Deceased agents remain "toxic" until cleared, requiring the player/system to manage "cleanup" to prevent further spread.

### 📉 Economic & Civil Mechanics
* **Supply Chain Integrity:** Calculated based on the ratio of healthy "Working Adults." A collapsing supply chain increases mortality and halts research.
* **Fear-Based Movement:** High death rates trigger "Public Panic," causing agents to flee hubs, which simulates social distancing but hurts the economy.
* **Dynamic Budgeting:** Funds are generated by the healthy population and automatically diverted to ICU expansion and Vaccine R&D.

### 🔭 Cinematic Telemetry
* **Orbital Surveillance:** A self-rotating 3D camera provides a comprehensive view of the agent population.
* **Live Dashboards:** Real-time tracking of Global Biometrics, Civil Infrastructure, and Medical Capabilities.
